Overview

  1. GSEC is GIAC’s foundational, hands-on security certification for professionals who need to show they understand security tasks beyond simple terminology and concepts.
  2. The exam has 106 questions in 4 hours, and the passing score is 72% for exam versions released on or after April 6, 2026 (check your GIAC account for the format and pass mark of your attempt).
  3. It’s a proctored, web-based exam taken remotely through ProctorU or at a Pearson VUE test center, and you have 120 days from activation to complete your attempt.
  4. GIAC exams are open book: you can bring printed books and notes and your own index, but no electronic material.
  5. GIAC publishes 26 certification objectives without weights, so this full-length test has 106 questions split evenly: 5 for each of the first two objectives and 4 for each of the rest.
  6. GSEC certification page for more details.
