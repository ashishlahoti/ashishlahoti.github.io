Overview

  1. Implementing and Operating Cisco Enterprise Network Core Technologies (ENCOR, 350-401) is the core exam for CCNP Enterprise and the qualifying exam for the CCIE Enterprise lab exams.
  2. The exam lasts 120 minutes; Cisco doesn’t publish the number of questions or the pass mark. This practice test has 100 questions and uses 80% as the target.
  3. The six v1.2 domains are weighted 15%, 10%, 30%, 10%, 20% and 15%, so this test has 100 questions split 15 / 10 / 30 / 10 / 20 / 15.
  4. Topics include enterprise design and high availability, Catalyst SD-WAN, SD-Access, QoS, hypervisors, VRF, GRE and IPsec, LISP, VXLAN, trunking, EtherChannel, RSTP and MST, EIGRP, OSPFv2/v3, eBGP, policy-based routing, NTP and PTP, NAT, HSRP and VRRP, multicast, debugs, syslog, SNMP, Flexible NetFlow, SPAN, IP SLA, Catalyst Center, NETCONF and RESTCONF, AAA, ACLs, CoPP, REST API security, TrustSec, MACsec, Python, JSON, YANG, EEM and orchestration tools.
