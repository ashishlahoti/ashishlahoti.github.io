Overview

  1. PSM I validates that you understand Scrum as described in the Scrum Guide and how to apply it in Scrum Teams — the exam tests precise adherence to the Guide’s exact wording, not “common practice” folklore.
  2. The exam consists of 80 questions in Multiple Choice, Multiple Answer, and True/False format.
  3. You have 60 minutes to complete the assessment, and a passing score of 85% is required.
  4. The exam costs $200 USD per attempt; test passwords have no expiration date but are valid for one attempt only.
  5. PSM I certification never expires — it is a lifetime certification with no annual renewal fee, and it includes a free Credly digital credential.
  6. There are no mandatory prerequisites and no required course attendance, though Scrum.org recommends attending a Professional Scrum Master or Applying Professional Scrum course.
  7. The authoritative content source for every question below is the Scrum Guide, current November 2020 version, authored by Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland — there is no newer official revision as of this writing.
  8. Scrum.org does not publish official domain-weight percentages for PSM I (unlike some vendor-specific exams); instead, PSM I questions are drawn from three Focus Areas defined in the Professional Scrum Competencies: Understanding and Applying the Scrum Framework, Developing People and Teams, and Managing Products with Agility.
  9. This guide instead organizes its 240 practice questions around the Scrum Guide’s own structure, since that document is the actual source of truth the exam draws from.
  10. Scrum.org’s own free Scrum Open assessment is an excellent official self-check tool to try before attempting the real exam.
  11. Official PSM I certification page for more details.
