Overview

  1. Tableau Data Analyst is for analysts who understand a business problem, find the right data to explore and deliver actionable insights with Tableau Desktop, Tableau Prep and Tableau Server or Tableau Cloud.
  2. The exam has 60 multiple-choice and multiple-select questions (plus up to 5 unscored) in 105 minutes. There is no prerequisite.
  3. Passing score is 65%. The registration fee is US$200 and a retake is US$100, plus applicable taxes.
  4. Questions are tested against Tableau version 2024.2, according to the exam guide.
  5. The four domains are weighted 24%, 41%, 26% and 9%, so each of the two practice tests has 60 questions split 14 / 25 / 16 / 5, with 5 multiple-select questions. Together they cover every objective in the exam guide.
  6. Exam guide for more details.
