Overview

  1. SnowPro Core is Snowflake’s foundational certification. It validates knowledge of the Snowflake AI Data Cloud: architecture, account management and governance, data loading and unloading, performance and querying, and data collaboration.
  2. The current exam is COF-C03, launched on 16 February 2026. Snowflake recommends at least 6 months of hands-on Snowflake experience; there are no formal prerequisites.
  3. The exam has 100 questions (multiple choice and multiple select) in 115 minutes.
  4. Passing score is 750 out of 1000 (scaled).
  5. The five domains are weighted at 31%, 20%, 18%, 21% and 10%. This test follows the same split, and 8 questions are multiple select.
  6. SnowPro Core is the prerequisite for the SnowPro Advanced certifications (such as Data Engineer and Architect), and the certification is valid for 2 years.
  7. SnowPro Core (COF-C03) certification page for more details.
