Overview

  1. The AIGP certification shows you can ensure safety and trust in the development, deployment and use of AI — it is aimed at AI governance, privacy, compliance, risk and product professionals.
  2. It covers AI governance foundations, how privacy, IP, nondiscrimination, consumer-protection and product-liability laws apply to AI, AI-specific law (the EU AI Act), standards and frameworks (NIST AI RMF, OECD, ISO/IEC 42001, 42005, 22989), and governing AI development and deployment.
  3. The exam fee is US$649 for IAPP members and US$799 for non-members (retake US$475 / US$625).
  4. The exam has 100 multiple choice questions (85 scored, 15 unscored), about 30% based on case studies, in 3 hours with an optional 15-minute break.
  5. The passing score is 300 on a 100–500 scale; this practice test uses 70% as the target. Our test also includes a few select-two items for extra practice.
  6. Body of Knowledge v2.1 sets 16–20, 19–23, 21–25 and 21–25 scored questions for the four domains, so this test’s 100 questions are split 21 / 25 / 27 / 27.
  7. The certification is valid for two years; maintain it with 20 CPE credits (non-members pay a US$250 maintenance fee).
  8. Body of Knowledge and candidate handbook for more details.
