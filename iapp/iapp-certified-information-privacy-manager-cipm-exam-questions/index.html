Free IAPP Certified Information Privacy Manager (CIPM) Online Practice Exam

Overview

  1. The IAPP Certified Information Privacy Manager (CIPM) shows you can build and run a privacy program: setting its framework and governance, assessing and protecting data, sustaining performance and responding to requests and incidents.
  2. The exam has 90 multiple-choice questions in 2.5 hours, including a 15-minute break, and costs US$550.
  3. IAPP exams are scored on a 100–500 scale with 300 to pass; this practice test uses 70% as the target.
  4. Body of Knowledge v4.2.0 (effective 1 September 2025) sets question ranges per domain; this test’s 75 questions are split 16 / 14 / 14 / 11 / 8 / 12, with 6 multiple-select questions, in 120 minutes.
  5. There are no prerequisites. Certification requires IAPP membership or a maintenance fee and continuing education to stay active.
  6. CIPM certification page and Body of Knowledge for more details.

Domain 1: Privacy Program: Developing a Framework (14–18 q)

A new privacy manager is setting the scope of the program. What should come first?

⬜ Buy a privacy management tool and configure every module before speaking to the business, so data owners can be onboarded
⬜ Understand what personal data the organization holds, where it comes from and how it’s used
⬜ Draft external privacy notices for every website before any data has been identified
⬜ Run an awareness campaign for all staff without knowing which laws apply

Which governance model places privacy decision-making in one central team for the whole organization?

⬜ Local, where each business unit sets its own privacy rules independently
⬜ Hybrid, combining a central policy team with local privacy champions in each region
⬜ Outsourced, where an external consultancy owns every privacy decision
⬜ Centralized

A global retailer with strong regional teams wants consistent standards but local execution. Which model fits?

⬜ Hybrid
⬜ Fully centralized, with every privacy task handled by headquarters staff only
⬜ Fully local, with each country choosing its own standards and tools
⬜ No formal model, letting each team decide case by case as issues come up

Which document states the privacy program’s purpose and goals for the organization?

⬜ Data processing agreement signed with each processor that handles customer data
⬜ Incident register listing every breach the organization has handled
⬜ A privacy vision and mission statement
⬜ Records of processing activities required by Article 30

Which group should the privacy manager identify as internal partners early?

⬜ Only the legal team, because privacy is a purely legal discipline
⬜ Stakeholders such as IT, security, HR, legal, marketing and procurement
⬜ Only external regulators, since they decide what the program must do and which teams should be involved in it
⬜ Only customers who have filed complaints in the past year

What is the main purpose of a privacy awareness campaign?

⬜ To replace formal training and policies with posters and newsletters only
⬜ To inform regulators about the organization’s internal culture
⬜ To collect marketing consent from employees for internal communications
⬜ To build a shared understanding of privacy across the organization

A company operates in the EU, California and Singapore. What must the privacy manager determine?

⬜ Which territorial, sectoral and industry rules apply in each place
⬜ Which single law to follow, since organizations may pick one jurisdiction for all their processing
⬜ Only the law of the country where the company’s servers are located
⬜ Whether a global privacy policy can replace all local legal requirements

Which is an example of a sectoral privacy law?

⬜ The EU General Data Protection Regulation, which applies across all sectors
⬜ Brazil’s LGPD, which applies broadly to all organizations
⬜ HIPAA for US health care
⬜ Japan’s APPI, a comprehensive national privacy law

Which certification lets companies demonstrate compliance with recognized cross-border privacy rules through an accountability agent?

⬜ ISO 9001 quality management certification issued by a national standards body
⬜ The Global CBPR system
⬜ PCI DSS compliance validated by a Qualified Security Assessor
⬜ SOC 1 reports on financial reporting controls

What is a consequence of noncompliance that a privacy manager should communicate to leadership?

⬜ Higher marketing conversion rates because data can be used more freely
⬜ Lower IT costs because fewer security controls are needed
⬜ Faster product launches with no downside for the organization
⬜ Fines, enforcement orders, litigation and reputational damage

Why should a privacy manager understand the authority of oversight agencies?

⬜ To know their powers, such as audits, orders and fines, and how to engage with them
⬜ To decide which regulators the organization can safely ignore because they rarely audit companies in its sector
⬜ Because regulators approve every processing activity in advance
⬜ To transfer accountability for compliance to the regulator

The business wants to use AI to score customer creditworthiness. Which privacy risk should the manager raise?

⬜ The model will run slower than the existing rules-based system in production
⬜ AI always requires more storage than manual processes
⬜ Automated decisions may be biased, opaque or affect individuals’ rights
⬜ Customers can’t be notified about AI use under any law

What does an organization’s risk appetite tell the privacy manager?

⬜ The number of privacy staff the company will hire next year and the budget approved for the team’s tooling
⬜ How much privacy risk leadership is willing to accept in pursuit of objectives
⬜ Which customers to prioritize for marketing campaigns
⬜ How fast the IT team can deploy new systems

Which statement about self-certification frameworks is correct?

⬜ They exempt organizations from all applicable privacy laws once certified, so no further compliance work is required by law
⬜ They are only available to government agencies
⬜ They are reviewed by customers rather than regulators or agents
⬜ They require the organization to commit to principles and can be enforced if it fails to follow them

Doing business in a country whose laws allow broad government access to data creates which concern?

⬜ Conflicts with other jurisdictions’ requirements and transfer restrictions
⬜ Lower product prices for customers in that country
⬜ Better data quality from local suppliers
⬜ No concern, because local law always overrides other jurisdictions

Which TWO elements help define the scope of a privacy program? (Select two)

⬜ Types and sources of personal data
⬜ The office’s furniture budget
⬜ Applicable laws and regulations
⬜ The colors of the company logo
⬜ The CEO’s travel schedule

Domain 2: Privacy Program: Establishing Program Governance (12–16 q)

Which policy explains how long each category of personal data is kept and how it’s disposed of?

⬜ Acceptable use policy for company devices and networks
⬜ Bring-your-own-device policy for employee phones
⬜ Data retention and disposal policy
⬜ Remote working policy for home offices

Which plan should exist before a breach occurs?

⬜ A plan to notify the media before investigating any incident
⬜ A data breach management plan
⬜ A plan to delete logs so attackers can’t read them
⬜ A plan to wait for customers to report problems

What should a complaint procedure provide?

⬜ A way to redirect all complaints to the regulator automatically, so the organization never handles them
⬜ A policy of ignoring anonymous complaints entirely
⬜ A requirement that complainants pay a fee before review
⬜ A clear channel to receive, track and resolve privacy complaints

Who should be accountable for each step of breach response?

⬜ Defined functions such as legal, security, communications and the privacy office
⬜ Whoever happens to discover the breach, without predefined roles, so the response can adapt to each situation
⬜ Only the external forensic firm hired after the incident
⬜ Only the CEO, who handles every task personally

Which metric best helps the board oversee the privacy program?

⬜ The number of words in the privacy notice
⬜ The number of privacy team meetings held each month
⬜ Trends in DSAR response times and open high-risk findings
⬜ The font used in policy documents

How should privacy law changes across jurisdictions be tracked?

⬜ Wait for regulators to send letters before reviewing any changes to the laws that apply to the business
⬜ Set up a regular monitoring process, such as legal alerts and periodic reviews
⬜ Check once when the program is launched and never again
⬜ Rely on vendors to tell the organization about new laws

Which training approach is most effective?

⬜ One generic annual video for everyone, regardless of role
⬜ Training only for the privacy team
⬜ Training only after a breach occurs
⬜ Targeted training based on roles and data handled

What do audits contribute to privacy governance?

⬜ Independent assurance that controls and policies work
⬜ They replace the need for policies and procedures
⬜ They are only required for public companies
⬜ They decide which laws apply

Which role manages internal and external data sharing decisions?

⬜ It’s left to individual employees to decide case by case, based on their own judgment about each request they receive
⬜ Only the marketing department
⬜ Defined roles in the privacy governance model, often with data owners and legal review
⬜ Only the external auditor

When designing data collection points, what should the privacy manager ensure?

⬜ Collection of as much data as possible for future use
⬜ Transparency at collection and good data quality
⬜ Hidden collection to reduce form abandonment
⬜ Copying all data to marketing systems by default

Which framework helps organizations structure privacy governance around Identify, Govern, Control, Communicate and Protect?

⬜ PCI DSS, the payment card security standard
⬜ ITIL service management practices
⬜ COBIT IT governance only
⬜ NIST Privacy Framework

Which standard extends ISO/IEC 27001 with a privacy information management system?

⬜ ISO/IEC 27701
⬜ ISO 9001
⬜ ISO 14001
⬜ ISO 22301

Where should the privacy team report to ensure independence and visibility?

⬜ Under the marketing department, so data use decisions are made quickly by the team that uses the most customer data
⬜ To an external vendor that manages the privacy tool
⬜ To a level with enough authority, such as senior leadership or a governance committee
⬜ To no one, so the team can act freely

Which TWO belong in a data subject rights process? (Select two)

⬜ Verifying the requester’s identity
⬜ Charging a fee for every request
⬜ Tracking deadlines for responses
⬜ Deleting the request after reading it
⬜ Requiring requests by fax only

Domain 3: Operational Life Cycle: Assessing Data (12–16 q)

What does a data inventory help a privacy manager do?

⬜ Replace the need for any privacy policies or procedures in the organization
⬜ Know what personal data exists, where it is and how it flows
⬜ Avoid documenting processing activities for regulators
⬜ Automatically comply with every privacy law worldwide

What is a gap analysis in a privacy program?

⬜ A survey of employee satisfaction with privacy training sessions, run each year and compared across departments
⬜ A count of missing laptops in the asset register
⬜ A test of network latency between data centers
⬜ A comparison of current practices with applicable laws and standards to find shortfalls

Before outsourcing payroll processing, what should the organization assess?

⬜ The vendor’s controls, contract terms and any international transfers
⬜ Only the vendor’s price and delivery speed, since privacy is the vendor’s problem
⬜ Only the vendor’s marketing materials and website
⬜ Nothing, if the vendor is a large company

Which physical control risk should be evaluated at an office?

⬜ Website loading speed for customers
⬜ The number of social media followers
⬜ Unlocked cabinets with printed customer files
⬜ The company’s tax rate

Old hard drives with customer data are being retired. What should happen?

⬜ Sell them as-is to recover some value
⬜ Sanitize or destroy them following a standard such as NIST SP 800-88
⬜ Store them in a hallway until someone decides what to do with them, as long as the building has badge access
⬜ Give them to employees for personal use

Which technical control assessment question matters most for cloud storage of personal data?

⬜ Whether the cloud provider’s logo matches the company brand
⬜ How many data centers the provider advertises
⬜ Whether the provider sponsors sports events
⬜ Where data is stored and whether it crosses borders

In a planned acquisition, what should the privacy team do during due diligence?

⬜ Review the target’s data, consents, contracts, breaches and compliance
⬜ Wait until after closing to look at the target’s privacy practices
⬜ Assume the target’s practices match the buyer’s
⬜ Copy all the target’s customer data to the buyer’s systems immediately

After an acquisition, customer data was collected under the target’s privacy notice. What must be checked before using it for the buyer’s marketing?

⬜ Only the size of the database
⬜ Whether the data is stored in the cloud
⬜ Whether the new use is compatible with the original notice and consent
⬜ Nothing; acquired data can be used freely by the buyer because the purchase transfers all rights to the data

At what level should vendor assessments be performed?

⬜ Always at the CEO level only
⬜ At the most appropriate functional level, based on the risk of the processing
⬜ Only by the vendor itself without review, since the vendor understands its own controls better than the customer
⬜ Never for low-cost vendors

Which document maps how personal data moves between systems, teams and vendors?

⬜ Organization chart
⬜ Office floor plan
⬜ Marketing calendar
⬜ Data flow map

What should be measured against internal and external requirements during assessment?

⬜ Policy compliance
⬜ Employee lunch preferences
⬜ Server rack colors
⬜ Office temperature

A system keeps customer data long after the purpose ends. What should the privacy manager do?

⬜ Keep it in case it becomes useful later
⬜ Move it to an employee’s personal drive
⬜ Set limits on use and retention and enforce deletion
⬜ Encrypt it and keep it forever

In a divestiture, part of the customer base moves to the buyer. What should be ensured?

⬜ The seller keeps a full copy of the transferred data forever, in case the buyer asks questions after the deal
⬜ Data is separated correctly and transferred under appropriate agreements
⬜ All data is transferred, including unrelated customers
⬜ No documentation is kept

Which TWO are part of documenting data governance systems? (Select two)

⬜ Data inventories
⬜ Office seating plans
⬜ System integrations and data flows
⬜ The company’s stock price
⬜ Employee birthdays

Domain 4: Operational Life Cycle: Protecting Personal Data (9–13 q)

Why classify data under a classification scheme?

⬜ To make files larger so they’re harder to steal
⬜ To replace access controls entirely
⬜ To let everyone access everything
⬜ To apply controls proportional to the data’s sensitivity

Which is an administrative control?

⬜ A clean-desk policy with staff training
⬜ Full-disk encryption on laptops
⬜ A firewall rule blocking a port
⬜ Multi-factor authentication tokens

What is a limitation of encryption as a control?

⬜ It never protects data at rest under any circumstances, only data in transit across networks
⬜ It’s illegal in all countries
⬜ It doesn’t protect data from authorized users who misuse it
⬜ It deletes data automatically

Which access control principle gives users only the access their role requires?

⬜ Open access for convenience across all teams
⬜ Least privilege
⬜ Seniority-based access for long-serving staff
⬜ Shared administrator accounts

Where should privacy be integrated according to Privacy by Design?

⬜ Only at the end of a project, during final testing before launch
⬜ Only in the privacy notice
⬜ Only in the legal contract with customers
⬜ Throughout the SDLC and business processes

A marketing team wants to reuse customer support data for targeted ads. What should the privacy manager verify?

⬜ That secondary-use guidelines and legal requirements are followed
⬜ That the ads will be profitable enough to justify the reuse of customer support data for marketing
⬜ That the data is stored on fast servers
⬜ That the support team agrees, without further review

Which privacy-enhancing technique should the manager ask technologists to apply to analytics data?

⬜ Publishing raw data so analysts can work faster
⬜ Copying data to personal laptops
⬜ Pseudonymization or aggregation to reduce identifiability
⬜ Removing all access controls

How can a privacy manager verify that vendor contracts are applied in practice?

⬜ Assume compliance once the contract is signed
⬜ Monitor through audits, questionnaires and evidence reviews
⬜ Rely on the vendor’s website claims
⬜ Ask employees if they trust the vendor

Which control helps ensure data classifications remain effective over time?

⬜ Classifying data once and never revisiting it
⬜ Removing classification labels after a year
⬜ Letting users change classifications freely
⬜ Periodic access reviews and classification checks

Which measure is organizational rather than technical?

⬜ Appointing data owners responsible for datasets
⬜ Tokenization of card numbers
⬜ Network segmentation
⬜ Encryption of backups

Which TWO are technical controls? (Select two)

⬜ Multi-factor authentication
⬜ Privacy awareness posters
⬜ Encryption of data at rest
⬜ A code of conduct
⬜ Annual training attendance records

Domain 5: Operational Life Cycle: Sustaining Program Performance (7–9 q)

Which metric shows whether the privacy program is improving over time?

⬜ A single snapshot of last month’s incidents, presented without comparison to earlier periods or targets
⬜ The length of the privacy policy
⬜ Trends in key indicators such as incidents and DSAR response times
⬜ The number of slides in the board deck

What is the purpose of a privacy audit?

⬜ To punish staff who made mistakes in the past and record their names in the annual compliance report
⬜ To assess policies, controls and standards against requirements and changes
⬜ To replace risk assessments
⬜ To approve the marketing budget

Which assessment is a short screening to decide whether a fuller PIA is needed?

⬜ Transfer impact assessment (TIA)
⬜ Legitimate interests assessment (LIA)
⬜ Data protection impact assessment (DPIA)
⬜ Privacy threshold analysis (PTA)

Which assessment evaluates whether relying on legitimate interests as a lawful basis is justified?

⬜ Legitimate interests assessment (LIA)
⬜ Transfer impact assessment
⬜ Privacy threshold analysis
⬜ Business continuity assessment

Which assessment examines whether a third country’s laws undermine safeguards for transferred data?

⬜ Legitimate interests assessment
⬜ Privacy threshold analysis
⬜ Transfer impact assessment (TIA)
⬜ Vendor price comparison

After a merger, two privacy programs must be combined. What should happen?

⬜ Keep both programs running separately forever, with different policies and teams in each company
⬜ Assess risks, align controls and communicate changes to stakeholders
⬜ Delete one company’s data
⬜ Ignore privacy until the next audit

How should audit types be chosen?

⬜ Always use external audits for everything, since internal audits are never considered independent enough
⬜ Randomly
⬜ Only when a regulator demands it
⬜ Based on program goals, such as internal control audits or subcontractor audits

Which TWO are valid privacy program metrics? (Select two)

⬜ Percentage of staff completing role-based training
⬜ Number of office plants
⬜ Average time to close data subject requests
⬜ Cafeteria menu changes
⬜ Parking spaces used

Domain 6: Operational Life Cycle: Responding to Requests and Incidents (10–14 q)

Under the GDPR, how long does an organization generally have to respond to an access request?

⬜ One month, extendable by two months in some cases
⬜ Seven days
⬜ Six months
⬜ There is no deadline

Under the CCPA, which right lets California consumers stop the sale or sharing of their personal information?

⬜ Right to rectification under the GDPR
⬜ Right to data portability only
⬜ Right to opt out of sale or sharing
⬜ Right to be informed of a breach

A customer withdraws consent for marketing emails. What should happen?

⬜ Continue emails until the next quarterly list cleanup, when all withdrawals are processed together in one batch
⬜ Stop processing for that purpose promptly and record the withdrawal
⬜ Ask the customer to explain why
⬜ Delete the customer’s account entirely

What should an organization do with a request whose requester’s identity is unclear?

⬜ Fulfil it immediately to avoid delay
⬜ Ignore it
⬜ Send the data to any email address provided
⬜ Request reasonable information to verify identity

What is the first step when a potential incident is reported?

⬜ Assess and contain it
⬜ Notify all customers immediately before knowing the facts
⬜ Delete affected logs
⬜ Wait to see if it happens again

Under the GDPR, when must a controller notify the supervisory authority of a breach?

⬜ Within 24 hours of any security event, even if no personal data was involved or affected by the incident at all
⬜ Within 30 days
⬜ Without undue delay, within 72 hours where feasible, unless the breach is unlikely to result in risk
⬜ Only if a journalist reports it

Under HIPAA, breaches affecting 500 or more individuals must be reported to HHS when?

⬜ At year-end with smaller breaches, in a single annual log submitted to HHS for all breaches regardless of size
⬜ Without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 days after discovery
⬜ Within 72 hours
⬜ Only if requested

Why maintain an incident register?

⬜ To meet marketing reporting needs and show customers how few incidents the company has each year
⬜ To replace notifications
⬜ To track office supplies
⬜ To document incidents, decisions and actions for accountability and regulators

What is the purpose of a post-incident review?

⬜ To identify lessons and update the response plan to reduce future impact
⬜ To assign blame to individuals
⬜ To close the file quickly
⬜ To delete evidence

Which stakeholder communication should an incident plan prepare in advance?

⬜ Social media posts blaming a vendor
⬜ A plan to say nothing
⬜ Templates for regulators, affected individuals and partners per jurisdiction
⬜ Press releases written after notification deadlines have passed, once the full facts are confirmed by forensics

A privacy notice lists rights but customers can’t find how to exercise them. What should be fixed?

⬜ Remove the rights section
⬜ Explain clearly how to exercise each right and provide easy channels
⬜ Require customers to visit an office in person with identification to learn how each right can be exercised
⬜ Make the notice longer

Which TWO are part of incident response? (Select two)

⬜ Containment
⬜ Marketing campaign launch
⬜ Remediation
⬜ Office redesign
⬜ Annual holiday planning
