Overview

  1. This is a foundations-level certification from Anthropic for knowledge workers who use Claude day to day: writing effective prompts, checking and validating Claude’s output, choosing the right model and feature (chat, web search, Research, Projects, artifacts, Skills, connectors), designing Claude into team workflows, configuring Projects, instructions, memory and knowledge, using Claude responsibly, and troubleshooting.
  2. It suits business users, analysts, coordinators and team leads with limited to moderate technical experience. There are no prerequisites.
  3. Exam code is CCAO-F, and passing it earns the Claude Certified Associate - Foundations credential with a Credly badge. The exam is proctored, online or at a Pearson VUE test center, and costs US$99 (partner discounts may apply).
  4. The exam has 60 questions (multiple choice and multiple response; each item says how many answers to choose) in 120 minutes.
  5. The passing score is 720 on a scaled score of 100–1,000, and the score report shows your result for each domain.
  6. The exam covers 7 domains (see the weighting table below), following exam guide version 1.0, effective July 2026. The credential is valid for 12 months and can be renewed with a free online renewal assessment. Claude’s features change often, so check the Help Center for the latest behavior.
  7. Anthropic announcement, Claude Help Center and Claude prompting best practices for more details.
