Free Microsoft Power Platform Fundamentals (PL-900) Online Practice Exam

Overview

  1. This is a Fundamentals level certification for people who want to show they understand how Microsoft Power Platform improves productivity, from business users to aspiring makers and developers.
  2. Passing PL-900 earns the Microsoft Certified: Power Platform Fundamentals credential. Fundamentals certifications don’t expire, so there’s no annual renewal. Cost varies by country/region.
  3. Exam duration is 45 minutes, with about 40 to 60 questions (multiple choice, multiple response and other item types).
  4. Passing score is 700 (out of 1000).
  5. The five domains are weighted 5–10%, 20–25%, 20–25%, 20–25% and 20–25%, so there are two practice tests of 50 questions, each split 4 / 12 / 11 / 11 / 12 with 2 multiple-select questions (100 questions in total).
  6. Study Guide and certification page for more details.
