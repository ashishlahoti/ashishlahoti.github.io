Free ISACA Certified Information Security Manager (CISM) Online Practice Exam

Overview

  1. CISM (Certified Information Security Manager) is ISACA’s management-focused security certification for people who design, manage and oversee an enterprise information security program.
  2. Certification requires 5 years of information security work experience, including 3 years in security management, gained within the 10 years before applying (or within 5 years after passing).
  3. The exam has 150 multiple-choice questions in 4 hours, at PSI test centers or remotely proctored. Questions ask for the BEST, MOST or FIRST answer from a manager’s point of view.
  4. Scores are scaled from 200 to 800, and 450 or higher passes; this practice test uses 70% as the target.
  5. The exam costs US$575 for ISACA members and US$760 for non-members.
  6. The four domains are weighted 18% / 20% / 33% / 29%, so this test has 150 questions split 27 / 30 / 50 / 43.
  7. Exam content outline and CISM certification page for more details.
