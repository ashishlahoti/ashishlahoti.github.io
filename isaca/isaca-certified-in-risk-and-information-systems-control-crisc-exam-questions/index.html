Overview

  1. CRISC (Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control) is ISACA’s certification for professionals who identify and manage IT and enterprise risk and design, implement and monitor information system controls.
  2. Certification requires 3 years of experience in IT risk management and IS control across the CRISC domains.
  3. The exam has 150 multiple-choice questions in 4 hours, at test centers or remotely proctored. Questions ask for the BEST, MOST or FIRST answer from a risk practitioner’s point of view.
  4. Scores are scaled from 200 to 800, and 450 or higher passes; this practice test uses 70% as the target.
  5. The four domains are weighted 26% / 22% / 32% / 20%, so this test has 150 questions split 39 / 33 / 48 / 30, with 9 multiple-select questions.
  6. Exam content outline and CRISC certification page for more details.
