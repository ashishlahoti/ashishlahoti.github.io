Free ISACA Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer (CDPSE) Online Practice Exam

Overview

  1. CDPSE (Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer) is ISACA’s certification for professionals who build privacy by design into systems, networks and applications.
  2. Certification requires at least 3 years of cumulative work experience across the four CDPSE domains, gained within the 10 years before applying.
  3. The exam has 120 multiple-choice questions in 3.5 hours, at PSI test centers or remotely proctored, in English only.
  4. Scores are scaled from 200 to 800, and 450 or higher passes; this practice test uses 70% as the target.
  5. The four domains are weighted 20% / 18% / 23% / 39%, so this test has 120 questions split 24 / 22 / 28 / 46, with 9 multiple-select questions.
  6. Exam content outline and CDPSE certification page for more details.
