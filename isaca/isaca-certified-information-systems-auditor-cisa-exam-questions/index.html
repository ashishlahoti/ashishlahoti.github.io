Overview

  1. CISA (Certified Information Systems Auditor) is ISACA’s globally recognized certification for professionals who audit, control, monitor and assess information technology and business systems.
  2. Certification requires 5 years of experience in IS audit, control, assurance or security; some education and other experience can substitute for part of it.
  3. The exam has 150 multiple-choice questions in 4 hours, at PSI test centers or remotely proctored. Questions ask for the BEST, MOST or FIRST answer from an auditor’s point of view.
  4. Scores are scaled from 200 to 800, and 450 or higher passes; this practice test uses 70% as the target.
  5. The exam costs US$575 for ISACA members and US$760 for non-members.
  6. The five domains are weighted 18% / 18% / 12% / 26% / 26%, so this test has 150 questions split 27 / 27 / 18 / 39 / 39.
  7. Exam content outline and CISA certification page for more details.
