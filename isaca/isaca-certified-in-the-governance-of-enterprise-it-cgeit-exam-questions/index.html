Overview

  1. CGEIT (Certified in the Governance of Enterprise IT) is ISACA’s certification for professionals who advise on or oversee the governance of enterprise IT: governance frameworks, IT strategy and resources, benefits realization and risk optimization.
  2. Certification requires five years of experience in an advisory or oversight role supporting the governance of IT-related contributions to the enterprise, including experience in Domain 1.
  3. The exam has 150 multiple-choice questions in 4 hours, at test centers or remotely proctored. Questions ask for the BEST, MOST or FIRST answer from a governance point of view. It costs US$575 for members and US$760 for non-members.
  4. Scores are scaled from 200 to 800, and 450 or higher passes; this practice test uses 70% as the target.
  5. The four domains are weighted 40% / 15% / 26% / 19%, so this test has 150 questions split 60 / 22 / 39 / 29, with 9 multiple-select questions.
  6. Exam content outline and CGEIT certification page for more details.
