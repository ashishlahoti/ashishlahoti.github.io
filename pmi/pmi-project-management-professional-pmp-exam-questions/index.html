Overview

  1. PMP (Project Management Professional) is PMI’s flagship certification for experienced project leaders, covering predictive, agile and hybrid ways of working.
  2. The exam has 180 questions (170 scored and 10 unscored pretest) in 240 minutes, with two 10-minute breaks.
  3. Question types include multiple choice (single and multiple response), matching, drag and drop, point and click, and new case/scenario and graphic-based questions.
  4. The current Exam Content Outline took effect in July 2026. It adds trends such as AI and sustainability, and reframes project success around stakeholder value and outcomes.
  5. About 40% of questions use predictive approaches and 60% use agile or hybrid approaches.
  6. The three domains are weighted 33%, 41% and 26%, so this test has 180 questions split 59 / 74 / 47.
  7. PMI doesn’t publish a pass mark; this practice test uses 70% as the target.
  8. PMP certification page and Exam Content Outline for more details.
