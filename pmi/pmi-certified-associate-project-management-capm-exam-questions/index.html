Overview

  1. CAPM (Certified Associate in Project Management) is PMI’s entry-level certification for people starting a project management career, covering predictive, agile and business analysis fundamentals.
  2. Eligibility needs a secondary degree (such as a high school diploma) and 23 hours of project management education before the exam.
  3. The exam has 150 questions (135 scored, 15 unscored pretest) in 180 minutes, with a 10-minute break after question 75.
  4. The four domains are weighted 36%, 17%, 20% and 27%, so this test has 150 questions split 54 / 25 / 30 / 41, with 10 multiple-select questions.
  5. PMI doesn’t publish a pass mark; this practice test uses 70% as the target.
  6. You maintain the certification by earning 15 PDUs every 3-year cycle.
  7. Examination Content Outline and certification page for more details.
