Free PMI Agile Certified Practitioner (PMI-ACP) Online Practice Exam

Overview

  1. PMI-ACP (Agile Certified Practitioner) validates agile knowledge and experience across approaches such as Scrum, Kanban, Lean and XP, rather than a single framework.
  2. Eligibility needs 2 years of agile experience in the past 5 years (or 1 year with a GAC-accredited degree or a third-party agile certification) and 21 hours of agile training.
  3. The exam has 120 multiple-choice questions (100 scored, 20 unscored pretest) in 180 minutes, with a 10-minute break after question 60.
  4. The four domains are weighted 28%, 25%, 19% and 28%, so this test has 120 questions split 34 / 30 / 23 / 33, with 8 multiple-select questions.
  5. PMI doesn’t publish a pass mark; this practice test uses 70% as the target.
  6. You maintain the certification by earning 30 PDUs every 3-year cycle.
  7. Examination Content Outline and certification page for more details.
