Overview

  1. This is a vendor-neutral, foundational cybersecurity certification, ideal for anyone pursuing roles such as security analyst, systems administrator, or network administrator.
  2. Requires 4 to 6 weeks of preparation depending upon your familiarity with networking and security concepts.
  3. Current exam code is SY0-701 (launched November 7, 2023; scheduled retirement June 11, 2027) and costs 404 USD per attempt (varies by country/region).
  4. You need to solve a maximum of 90 questions, a mix of multiple-choice and performance-based questions (PBQs), in 90 minutes of testing time.
  5. Passing score is 750 (on a scale of 100-900). There is no penalty for guessing, so answer every question!
  6. Question format includes multiple choice and performance-based questions that test hands-on, scenario-based problem solving rather than just recall.
  7. There are no formal prerequisites, though CompTIA recommends CompTIA Network+ plus about two years of IT experience with a security focus.
  8. The certification is valid for 3 years and must be renewed through CompTIA’s Continuing Education (CE) program (earning CEUs or a higher-level certification) or by retaking the current exam.
  9. Official exam page for more details.
