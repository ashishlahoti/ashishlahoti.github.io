Overview

  1. This is a vendor-neutral networking certification that validates the skills to design, implement, manage and troubleshoot wired and wireless networks, ideal for roles such as network administrator, network support technician or NOC technician.
  2. Requires 4 to 8 weeks of preparation depending upon your familiarity with networking concepts, especially subnetting and ports.
  3. Current exam code is N10-009 (launched June 20, 2024; retirement expected in 2027).
  4. You need to solve a maximum of 90 questions, a mix of multiple-choice and performance-based questions (PBQs), in 90 minutes of testing time.
  5. Passing score is 720 (on a scale of 100-900). There is no penalty for guessing, so answer every question!
  6. There are no formal prerequisites, though CompTIA recommends CompTIA A+ plus 9 to 12 months of hands-on experience as a junior network administrator or network support technician.
  7. The certification is valid for 3 years and can be renewed through CompTIA’s Continuing Education (CE) program.
  8. Official exam page for more details.
