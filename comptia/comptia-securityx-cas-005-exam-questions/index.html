Disclaimer: CompTIA is a protected Brand. These exam questions are neither endorsed by nor affiliated with CompTIA. These are not the official CompTIA exam questions/dumps. These questions are created from the CompTIA SecurityX (CAS-005) exam objectives, NIST publications (CSF 2.0, SP 800-207, SP 800-61, SP 800-218, FIPS 203/204), MITRE ATT&CK, OWASP, FIRST CVSS and CISA, and every explanation links to its source. Once you go through these questions and their concepts, you are more than ready to crack the exam in first attempt.

Note: SecurityX (CAS-005) is the expert-level exam formerly called CASP+. It’s vendor-neutral and scenario-based, so the questions focus on choosing the best design, control or response for a situation, including newer topics such as AI adoption risks, zero trust and post-quantum cryptography.

Free CompTIA SecurityX (CAS-005) Online Practice Exam

Overview

  1. The CompTIA SecurityX (CAS-005) certification, formerly CASP+, validates expert-level skills to architect, engineer and operate enterprise security across on-premises, cloud and hybrid environments.
  2. CompTIA recommends at least 10 years of general hands-on IT experience, including 5 years of broad hands-on security experience. There are no formal prerequisites.
  3. The exam has up to 90 questions (multiple-choice and performance-based) in 165 minutes. It’s pass/fail only, with no scaled score.
  4. The four domains are weighted 20%, 27%, 31% and 22%, so this test has 60 questions split 12 / 16 / 19 / 13, with 7 multiple-select questions. The online version runs at the real exam’s pace (60 questions in 110 minutes) with a 75% pass mark.
  5. Topics include CSF 2.0, risk analysis (ALE, risk responses, RTO/RPO), threat modeling, AI and LLM risks, zero trust, resilient and cloud architecture (CASB, CSPM, SASE), secure SDLC, federation and phishing-resistant MFA, IAM and network troubleshooting, TPM and OT security, automation, post-quantum and applied cryptography, SIEM analysis, vulnerability prioritization, threat hunting, threat intelligence and incident response.
  6. Exam objectives for more details.

Domain 1: Governance, Risk, and Compliance (20%)

A CISO must present the organization’s cybersecurity program to the board using outcomes such as Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover. Which framework uses these six functions?

⬜ ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A
⬜ PCI DSS v4.0
⬜ MITRE ATT&CK
⬜ NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0

A risk assessment estimates a server outage would cost $50,000 per incident and is expected twice a year. What is the annualized loss expectancy (ALE)?

⬜ $25,000
⬜ $50,000
⬜ $100,000
⬜ $200,000

After evaluating a risk, leadership decides to buy cyber insurance to cover potential losses from ransomware. Which risk response is this?

⬜ Accept
⬜ Transfer
⬜ Avoid
⬜ Mitigate

An organization expands into the EU and will process residents’ personal data. Which effect is this most likely to have on its security strategy?

⬜ Comply with GDPR obligations
⬜ It must comply only with the laws of its home country
⬜ It must store all data in plain text for regulator access
⬜ It no longer needs a data retention policy

A security architect wants to model threats against a new payment API by asking how an attacker could spoof identity, tamper with data, repudiate actions, disclose information, deny service or elevate privileges. Which methodology is this?

⬜ DREAD
⬜ STRIDE
⬜ PASTA
⬜ CVSS

A threat intelligence team wants to map an adversary’s observed behaviors, such as initial access, persistence and lateral movement, to a common knowledge base. Which resource fits best?

⬜ CVSS
⬜ NIST SP 800-88
⬜ ISO 22301
⬜ MITRE ATT&CK

A company plans to let employees use a public generative AI chatbot for work. Which TWO risks should governance address first? (Choose two.)

⬜ Employees pasting confidential data into prompts
⬜ The chatbot’s color scheme not matching the corporate brand
⬜ Inaccurate or fabricated outputs being used in decisions
⬜ Employees typing prompts too slowly to be productive

An LLM-based support assistant reads customer emails. An attacker sends an email containing hidden instructions that make the assistant reveal other customers’ data. Which risk is this?

⬜ SQL injection
⬜ Cross-site scripting
⬜ Model inversion via GPU side channels
⬜ Prompt injection

A company’s security policy states that all laptops must use full-disk encryption. Where would the specific approved encryption products and configuration settings normally be documented?

⬜ In the policy itself, in full technical detail
⬜ In the employee onboarding welcome letter
⬜ In the incident response plan
⬜ In a standard or baseline under the policy

A critical software vendor is acquired by a company in a high-risk jurisdiction. What should the organization do first?

⬜ Immediately uninstall the vendor’s software everywhere
⬜ Ignore it, since the software hasn’t changed
⬜ Reassess the supplier’s risk
⬜ Ask the vendor to publish its source code online

Which TWO metrics best show the board whether the security operations program is improving? (Choose two.)

⬜ The total count of log lines collected each day
⬜ Mean time to respond (MTTR) to incidents
⬜ Mean time to detect (MTTD) incidents
⬜ The number of firewall rules in the configuration

A business impact analysis finds a system can be down for at most 4 hours and lose at most 15 minutes of data. Which pair of values does this define?

⬜ RPO of 4 hours and RTO of 15 minutes
⬜ MTBF of 4 hours and MTTR of 15 minutes
⬜ RTO of 4 hours and RPO of 15 minutes
⬜ SLA of 4 hours and SLO of 15 minutes

Domain 2: Security Architecture (27%)

A company is adopting zero trust. Which principle best reflects the NIST zero trust architecture?

⬜ Decide access per session from identity and posture
⬜ Trust every device once it’s connected to the corporate LAN
⬜ Rely on a single strong perimeter firewall for protection
⬜ Give users broad access so they don’t need to reauthenticate

In the NIST zero trust model, which component makes the decision to grant, deny or revoke access to a resource?

⬜ The policy engine (PE)
⬜ The policy enforcement point (PEP)
⬜ The data plane switch
⬜ The certificate revocation list

Which approach best protects a global e-commerce site against the failure of an entire cloud region?

⬜ A larger single server in one availability zone
⬜ Active-active multi-region deployment
⬜ Nightly backups stored in the same region
⬜ Vertical scaling of the database during peak hours

A development team wants to catch vulnerable open source libraries before code is merged. Which control fits best in the CI pipeline?

⬜ A web application firewall in front of production
⬜ An annual penetration test of the production site
⬜ SCA that fails builds on vulnerable libraries
⬜ Manual review of every dependency once a year

Which TWO practices from the Secure Software Development Framework help protect software integrity? (Choose two.)

⬜ Keep provenance data such as an SBOM
⬜ Sign releases and protect signing keys
⬜ Disable code review to speed up releases
⬜ Let all developers push directly to production

A company wants employees to log in once and access many SaaS applications, with the identity provider sending signed assertions to each app. Which technology fits best?

⬜ Shared local accounts on each SaaS application
⬜ A spreadsheet of passwords shared securely
⬜ Federated single sign-on using SAML or OIDC tokens
⬜ Kerberos tickets sent over the internet to each app

A bank wants to stop phishing attacks that steal one-time codes. Which authenticator is most resistant to phishing?

⬜ FIDO2/WebAuthn passkeys bound to the site’s origin
⬜ SMS one-time passwords
⬜ Email one-time links
⬜ Security questions

A hospital wants doctors to access patient records only when they are on shift, on a managed device and treating that patient. Which access control model fits best?

⬜ Attribute-based access control (ABAC)
⬜ Discretionary access control (DAC)
⬜ Mandatory access control (MAC) labels only
⬜ Role-based access control with a single doctor role

Under the cloud shared responsibility model, who is responsible for configuring IAM policies and encrypting data in an IaaS deployment?

⬜ The cloud provider
⬜ The internet service provider
⬜ The hardware manufacturer
⬜ The customer

A security team needs visibility and policy control over employees’ use of sanctioned and unsanctioned SaaS applications. Which technology fits best?

⬜ A hardware security module (HSM)
⬜ A network tap on the data center core
⬜ A host-based firewall on each server
⬜ A cloud access security broker (CASB)

A cloud team wants to find misconfigurations such as public storage buckets and overly permissive security groups across all accounts continuously. Which tool category fits best?

⬜ Cloud security posture management (CSPM)
⬜ Endpoint detection and response (EDR)
⬜ Static application security testing (SAST)
⬜ A web application firewall (WAF)

An architect places a reverse proxy, WAF and DDoS protection in front of a public web application, and only the proxy can reach the app servers. Which design principle does this apply?

⬜ Defense in depth with segmentation
⬜ Security through obscurity
⬜ Single sign-on
⬜ Fail open

Which TWO capabilities are typical of a secure access service edge (SASE) architecture? (Choose two.)

⬜ Cloud-delivered secure web gateway (SWG)
⬜ Physical badge readers at each data center entrance
⬜ Zero trust network access (ZTNA) for private apps
⬜ On-premises tape backup for every branch office

A company must prevent sensitive design documents from being uploaded to personal cloud storage. Which control addresses this most directly?

⬜ Data loss prevention (DLP) policies on endpoints and web traffic
⬜ Full-disk encryption on all laptops
⬜ A stronger password policy
⬜ More frequent vulnerability scans

A team runs containers in production. Which practice most reduces the attack surface of each container image?

⬜ Install debugging tools in every image for faster troubleshooting
⬜ Run every container as root to avoid permission errors
⬜ Use the latest tag so images update automatically
⬜ Minimal, patched, non-root images

An organization uses infrastructure as code. How can it prevent insecure configurations from reaching the cloud?

⬜ Let engineers fix misconfigurations manually after deployment
⬜ Policy-as-code scans in CI
⬜ Disable version control for IaC to reduce complexity
⬜ Grant the pipeline full administrator rights everywhere

Domain 3: Security Engineering (31%)

Users report that SAML single sign-on to a SaaS app suddenly fails for everyone with “invalid signature” errors. What is the most likely cause?

⬜ Users entered their passwords incorrectly
⬜ The IdP rotated its SAML signing certificate
⬜ The SaaS app’s web server ran out of disk space
⬜ The users’ laptops need new screen savers

An OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect integration rejects tokens with an “invalid audience” error. What should the engineer check first?

⬜ Whether the user’s password has expired
⬜ Whether the API server’s clock is set to UTC
⬜ Whether the token was sent over HTTP instead of FTP
⬜ Whether the token’s aud claim matches the API

Which endpoint technology records process, file, registry and network activity, detects suspicious behavior and lets responders isolate a host remotely?

⬜ Endpoint detection and response (EDR)
⬜ Host-based firewall
⬜ Full-disk encryption
⬜ Mobile device management (MDM)

Which TWO techniques harden a Linux server against exploitation? (Choose two.)

⬜ Allow root to log in over SSH with a password
⬜ Enable SELinux or AppArmor in enforcing mode
⬜ Disable automatic security updates permanently
⬜ Remove unneeded services and packages

Users can reach a web app by IP address but not by its hostname, and only from one office. Which troubleshooting step makes the most sense first?

⬜ Replace the web server’s TLS certificate
⬜ Check DNS resolution
⬜ Reboot every laptop in the office
⬜ Open all ports on the firewall

After a firewall change, users can start TLS connections to a partner API but transfers stall when large responses arrive. What is a likely cause?

⬜ The partner’s DNS records were deleted
⬜ A path MTU problem, such as blocked ICMP or MSS clamping issues across a VPN
⬜ The TLS certificate expired years ago
⬜ The users’ accounts were disabled

Which hardware component stores measurements of the boot process so a remote verifier can confirm a device booted untampered firmware and OS?

⬜ A RAID controller
⬜ A graphics processing unit (GPU)
⬜ A network interface card offload engine
⬜ A Trusted Platform Module (TPM) used for measured boot and attestation

A manufacturer must keep running a Windows XP–based controller on its plant floor because the vendor no longer supports it. What’s the best way to reduce risk?

⬜ Connect it directly to the internet so the vendor can reach it
⬜ Install the latest consumer antivirus and leave it as is
⬜ Give all employees remote desktop access to it
⬜ Isolate it in a segmented zone with allowlisting

In an industrial control environment, which priority usually ranks highest when designing security controls?

⬜ Confidentiality of engineering emails
⬜ Safety and availability of the process
⬜ Speed of applying every patch immediately
⬜ Maximizing the number of remote connections

A SOC wants to automatically enrich phishing alerts, check URLs against threat intelligence, and quarantine emails when they’re malicious. Which technology fits best?

⬜ A hardware security module
⬜ A file integrity monitor
⬜ Security orchestration, automation and response (SOAR) playbooks
⬜ A network load balancer

Which statement about post-quantum cryptography is correct?

⬜ Doubling RSA key length makes it fully quantum-resistant
⬜ Quantum computers break all symmetric encryption, including AES-256
⬜ Post-quantum algorithms are only needed after 2050
⬜ NIST standardized ML-KEM (FIPS 203) for key exchange

Why are organizations concerned about “harvest now, decrypt later” attacks?

⬜ Attackers can decrypt TLS traffic in real time today with quantum computers
⬜ Today’s captures may be decrypted later
⬜ Hashing algorithms will stop producing consistent outputs
⬜ Encrypted backups will become corrupted over time

Which cryptographic technique lets a cloud service compute on encrypted data without decrypting it?

⬜ Tokenization
⬜ Homomorphic encryption
⬜ Key stretching
⬜ Steganography

A payment system must protect cardholder data in its database while still allowing systems to reference each record. Which technique replaces card numbers with non-sensitive values that map back only through a secure vault?

⬜ Tokenization
⬜ Hashing with MD5
⬜ Base64 encoding
⬜ Data compression

Which TWO practices strengthen TLS configuration for a public web service? (Choose two.)

⬜ Allow export-grade cipher suites for older clients
⬜ Disable TLS 1.0 and 1.1 and prefer TLS 1.3
⬜ Use self-signed certificates for all public sites
⬜ Use forward-secret key exchange such as ECDHE

A company wants to keep encryption keys for its cloud data under its own control, so the provider can’t access them without permission. Which approach fits?

⬜ Provider-managed default keys with no customer control
⬜ Storing keys in the same bucket as the encrypted data
⬜ Customer-managed keys in an HSM-backed key service
⬜ Hard-coding keys in application source code

An engineer automates patch deployment across thousands of servers with configuration management scripts. What should be built in to reduce risk?

⬜ Deploying to every server at once to finish faster
⬜ Staged rollouts with health checks and rollback
⬜ Skipping change management because it’s automated
⬜ Running the scripts with shared root passwords

Which use case is the best fit for a digital signature?

⬜ Hiding the contents of an email from everyone but the recipient
⬜ Compressing log files to save storage space
⬜ Proving an update is authentic and unmodified
⬜ Generating random session IDs for web users

Why might an engineer choose elliptic curve cryptography (ECC) over RSA for IoT devices?

⬜ ECC is immune to all quantum computing attacks
⬜ ECC doesn’t need any private keys to be protected
⬜ Comparable security with much smaller key sizes
⬜ RSA can’t be used for digital signatures

Domain 4: Security Operations (22%)

A SIEM shows a user account logging in from New York and, 10 minutes later, from Singapore. What does this pattern most likely indicate?

⬜ A normal VPN reconnection with no risk
⬜ A misconfigured printer on the network
⬜ Impossible travel suggesting compromised credentials
⬜ A successful patch deployment

A vulnerability scanner reports hundreds of findings. Which factors should most influence remediation priority?

⬜ The alphabetical order of the affected hostnames
⬜ Severity, exploitation, criticality, exposure
⬜ The date the scanner software was installed
⬜ Which team has the fewest open tickets

Which CVSS metric group describes the intrinsic characteristics of a vulnerability that don’t change over time or across environments?

⬜ Threat metrics
⬜ Base metrics
⬜ Environmental metrics
⬜ Supplemental metrics

Web server logs show requests like /products?id=1' OR '1'='1. Which attack is being attempted?

⬜ SQL injection
⬜ Cross-site request forgery
⬜ Directory traversal
⬜ Server-side request forgery

Endpoint logs show powershell.exe -nop -w hidden -enc <long base64 string> launched by a Word document. What should the analyst conclude?

⬜ Normal behavior from a routine Office update
⬜ A harmless user customizing their desktop
⬜ A backup job compressing user files
⬜ Likely malicious macro activity

A threat hunter starts with the hypothesis that attackers are using scheduled tasks for persistence, then searches telemetry for unusual task creation. Which approach is this?

⬜ Hypothesis-driven threat hunting
⬜ Signature-based antivirus scanning
⬜ Automated vulnerability scanning
⬜ Compliance auditing

Two organizations want to share threat intelligence in a structured, machine-readable format over an automated transport. Which standards fit?

⬜ SNMP for the format and SMTP for transport
⬜ CVSS for the format and FTP for transport
⬜ STIX for the data format and TAXII for transport
⬜ Syslog for the format and Telnet for transport

During an incident, which order of evidence collection follows the order of volatility?

⬜ Archived backups, then disk, then memory, then CPU cache
⬜ Disk, then backups, then memory, then registers
⬜ Registers and cache, memory, disk, then backups
⬜ Any order, since all evidence is equally volatile

Which TWO indicators in network logs suggest command-and-control (C2) beaconing? (Choose two.)

⬜ Long DNS queries with random-looking subdomains
⬜ Scheduled software updates from the vendor’s known update servers
⬜ Fixed-interval outbound connections to a rare domain
⬜ A single user opening the corporate intranet homepage once

An analyst finds a file whose hash matches known ransomware on one workstation. What should they do first, according to incident response best practice?

⬜ Contain the host with EDR while preserving evidence
⬜ Delete the file and close the ticket
⬜ Reimage the workstation immediately before collecting anything
⬜ Email the whole company about the infection

During memory analysis, an analyst finds a process with no file on disk, injected into a legitimate system process. What technique does this suggest?

⬜ A corrupted hard drive sector
⬜ A normal operating system update
⬜ A misconfigured screen resolution
⬜ Fileless malware using process injection

A SIEM rule generates hundreds of false positives every day, and analysts start ignoring it. What is the best response?

⬜ Disable all SIEM alerts permanently
⬜ Hire more analysts to review every alert
⬜ Tune the rule with better context and conditions
⬜ Lower the alert severity to informational and keep it

Which artifact best helps reconstruct what commands an attacker ran on a compromised Linux server?

⬜ Shell history, auth logs and auditd records
⬜ The server’s hardware warranty and purchase documents
⬜ The data center’s HVAC temperature logs
⬜ The company’s public website analytics
