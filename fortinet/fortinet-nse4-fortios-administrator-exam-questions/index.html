Overview

  1. The Fortinet NSE 4 - FortiOS 7.6 Administrator exam (FOS-ADM-7.6) validates the skills to deploy, configure and troubleshoot FortiGate firewalls.
  2. The exam has 50 to 55 questions in 100 minutes, in English and Japanese, based on FortiOS 7.6.0. Questions are multiple choice and often include exhibits: CLI or debug output, configuration extracts and troubleshooting scenarios, so many questions here include exhibits too.
  3. Fortinet doesn’t publish the pass mark in the exam description; this practice test uses 70% as the target.
  4. The five topics are weighted 20–25%, 20–25%, 25–30%, 10–15% and 10–15%, so there are three practice tests of 55 questions, each split 12 / 12 / 15 / 8 / 8 (165 questions in total).
  5. Topics include transparent mode, FGCP HA, administrative access, conserve mode and the packet sniffer, policy order, VIPs and IP pools, FSSO, SSL certificate and full inspection, inspection modes, static URL filtering, application control, distance and priority, policy routes, RPF, SD-WAN, IPsec troubleshooting, dial-up VPN and ADVPN, plus logging and FortiAnalyzer, FortiGuard and firmware, HA operation and upgrades, FortiGate CNF and FortiSASE, LDAP and RADIUS authentication, antivirus, IPS, web filter categories and quotas, ECMP, SD-WAN rules and SLAs, and redundant IPsec VPNs.
