Comprehensive list of Free ISC2 AI Security Certification practice questions, grouped by the six domains of the pilot exam outline, curated for getting ready for ISC2’s new AI security exam.

Disclaimer: ISC2 is a registered mark of ISC2, Inc. These questions are neither endorsed by nor affiliated with ISC2. These are not the official ISC2 exam questions/dumps. They are created from the public AI Security Pilot Exam Outline and public sources such as the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2025, NIST AI 100-2 and the AI RMF, MITRE ATLAS, the EU AI Act and CISA guidance, and every explanation links to its source.

Note: This certification is still in its pilot stage. ISC2 published the pilot exam outline on October 26, 2026; selected volunteers sit the pilot exam from mid-December 2026 to January 24, 2027, and the operational exam is expected in 2027. The name, domains and format may change before launch, and we will update these questions when they do.

Not the same as the AI Security Academy: ISC2 also runs the AI Security Academy, which offers certificates, courses and workshops across three levels (Foundations, Practitioner and Leader). These earn completion badges, not a certification, and they have no exam. The ISC2 AI Security Certification covered here is a separate credential earned by passing a standardized exam, so the questions below prepare you for that exam, not for the Academy certificates.

Free ISC2 AI Security Certification (Pilot) Online Practice Exam

Overview

  1. ISC2 is developing a vendor-neutral AI security certification for professionals who secure, govern and operate AI systems; it will be ISC2’s tenth certification.
  2. The pilot exam has 160 multiple-choice items in 3 hours at Pearson VUE test centers, in English; ISC2 hasn’t published a passing score yet.
  3. The pilot outline asks for 4 years of cumulative paid experience in cybersecurity or AI-related fields, including 2 years in cybersecurity and 1 year across two or more domains; an active CISSP, ISSAP, ISSEP, ISSMP, CCSP or CSSLP satisfies the whole requirement.
  4. This practice test is full length: 160 questions in 180 minutes, the same as the pilot exam, with a 70% target.
  5. The six domains are weighted 10%, 17%, 19%, 18%, 19% and 17%, so this test has 160 questions split 16 / 27 / 30 / 29 / 31 / 27, with 15 multiple-select questions.
  6. Pilot exam outline and ISC2 AI security certification page for more details.
