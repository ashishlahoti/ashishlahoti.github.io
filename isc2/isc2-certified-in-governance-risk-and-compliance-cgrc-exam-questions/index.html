Overview

  1. CGRC (Certified in Governance, Risk and Compliance) validates the skills to authorize and maintain information systems using governance, risk and compliance frameworks such as the NIST Risk Management Framework.
  2. Full certification needs 2 years of cumulative, paid work experience in one or more of the seven domains; without it you can become an Associate of ISC2.
  3. The exam has 125 items in 3 hours, multiple choice and advanced item types; this practice test is full length: 125 questions in 3 hours, the same as the real exam.
  4. The passing grade is 700 out of 1000 points; this practice test uses 70% as the target.
  5. The seven domains are weighted 16%, 10%, 14%, 17%, 16%, 14% and 13%, so this test has 125 questions split 20 / 13 / 17 / 21 / 20 / 18 / 16, with 8 multiple-select questions.
  6. Exam outline and certification page for more details.
